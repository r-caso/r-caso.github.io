abstract: >-
  In this chapter I propose an account of slurs according to which they have both a truth-conditional semantic dimension and a non-truth-conditional one. Following an insight by Kirk-Giannini (2019) and Lo Guercio (2020), I take it that the semantic type of a slur’s non-truth-conditional contribution is a property, or more properly a particular kind of de se proposition. My account, however, differs from theirs in both technical implementation and substantial issues, and has an independent justification.
