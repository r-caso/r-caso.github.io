abstract: >-
  We distinguish between experiential TAPs (‘intense’, ‘sombre’) and theoretical TAPs (‘balanced’, ‘dynamic’), and argue that there is evidence for a relativistic construal both at the truth-conditional and the expressive level for experiential TAPs, but not for theoretical TAPs. Thus, an upper bound is found on how much assessment sensitivity characterises aesthetic discourse.
