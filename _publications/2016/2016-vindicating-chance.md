abstract: >-
  The debate between reductionist and non-reductionist accounts of chance is presented, and the dialectical burdens acquired by each position are identified: the motivation problem and the explanation problem. It is argued that non-reductionists can successfully answer the explanation problem, whereas reductionists are unable to make the corresponding claim. Hence, the non-reductionist side has an important dialectical advantage.
